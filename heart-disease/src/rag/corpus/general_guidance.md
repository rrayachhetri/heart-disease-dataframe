# General Guidance for CardioSense Users

## How to use a CardioSense result
A CardioSense score is a conversation starter with a clinician, not a final
answer. Useful next steps after any result:
- Share the risk level, probability, and "top factors" list with a primary
  care physician or cardiologist.
- Ask your doctor whether additional testing (bloodwork, ECG, stress test,
  imaging) is appropriate given your personal and family history.
- Keep taking any prescribed medications and follow existing treatment
  plans regardless of this score.

## When to seek urgent/emergency care
Regardless of any CardioSense result, seek emergency care immediately for:
- Chest pain or pressure lasting more than a few minutes, or that comes and
  goes
- Chest discomfort spreading to the arm, back, neck, jaw, or stomach
- Shortness of breath with or without chest discomfort
- Cold sweat, nausea, or lightheadedness along with chest discomfort
These are classic warning signs of a possible heart attack and require
calling emergency services - not waiting for an app-based score.

## Commonly modifiable cardiovascular risk factors
General, well-established lifestyle factors associated with cardiovascular
risk (for general education only, not personalized medical advice):
- Smoking/tobacco use
- Physical inactivity
- Elevated blood pressure
- Elevated cholesterol, especially LDL
- Uncontrolled blood sugar / diabetes
- Excess body weight, particularly abdominal obesity
- Poor sleep and chronic stress

## Disclaimer
CardioSense, including this assistant, is an educational and research tool.
It is not a certified medical device and must not be used as a substitute
for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are
experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number
immediately.
